Work Schedule System

A workforce scheduling platform that handles shift assignments, attendance tracking, schedule change requests, and team calendar views — designed to reduce scheduling conflicts and give managers full visibility over their workforce.

Overview

Scheduling without the chaos

Managers were manually building shift schedules in spreadsheets, leading to conflicts, miscommunications, and last-minute gaps in coverage. Employees had no clear way to view their schedules or request changes without going through their supervisor directly.

The Work Schedule System was designed to give managers a structured, visual way to build and manage schedules — and give employees transparency and self-service control over their own time.

Key Modules

What I designed

Team Calendar View

Weekly and monthly calendar views showing assigned shifts per employee — with color-coded shift types, absence indicators, and team coverage at a glance.

Shift Assignment

Drag-and-assign interface for managers to build schedules — with conflict detection, minimum coverage alerts, and bulk assignment tools.

Attendance Monitoring

Daily time-in/out logs, late and absent flags, overtime tracking, and exportable attendance reports per department or individual.

Schedule Change Requests

Employee-submitted shift swap and schedule change requests — with manager approval flow, reason tracking, and notification system.

Notifications & Reminders

Automated shift reminders, approval status updates, and scheduling alerts sent to both managers and employees.

Manager Dashboard

Summary of today's coverage, pending requests, absent employees, and overtime hours — giving managers a daily operational snapshot.

Outcome

Fewer conflicts, better coverage

The system eliminated manual scheduling spreadsheets and reduced scheduling conflicts. Managers could build and adjust schedules in minutes, employees had full visibility into their shifts, and HR had accurate attendance data without manual compilation.
